Short version

  • The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress is stored only on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads ("Double treats", "Second chance") through Google AdMob. Ads only play when you tap one of these buttons.
  • To show and measure those ads, Google collects some device data, described below.
  • The Android app also sends anonymous usage statistics (for example which level was played and won) to Google Firebase Analytics, only where the consent form allows it.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, treats, upgrades, settings) is saved in the app's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • the device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads requested, shown, completed, tapped) and diagnostic data.

Purposes: delivering ads, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud and abuse, and – only with your consent – personalising ads. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated reports.

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

The Android app uses Firebase Analytics, provided by Google Ireland Limited (address above), to learn how the game is played so we can balance levels and fix problems. It records anonymous events such as: a level was started, won, lost or left (level number, stars, wave reached, lives left, Hard or daily mode), whether a rewarded ad was watched, plus standard app information (app version, device model, operating system, language, country, first open and session length). To tell apart installations without knowing who you are, Firebase uses a random app instance ID that is reset when you reinstall the app. We do not send your name, email, advertising ID or game save, and we only see aggregated reports.

Statistics are only collected where the consent form allows it: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app shows Google's consent form on first launch (the same form as for ads) and collects nothing until it has been answered; you can change your choice at any time via Privacy options in the game's menu. Elsewhere, where no consent is required, statistics are collected from the start.

Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK, Switzerland: on first launch the app shows Google's consent form. You can accept or refuse personalised ads. Refusing still allows non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time via Privacy options in the game's menu.
  • iOS: the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the IDFA is not shared.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • You never have to watch an ad to play the game.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Personalised advertising and access to your device's advertising identifier: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021). Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and ad measurement: our legitimate interest in financing the free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Usage statistics: your consent through the consent form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021). Google may transfer data outside the EU; it relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection, and to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. As we hold no personal data ourselves, requests regarding ad and statistics data are best directed to Google; you can also contact us at the address above. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
